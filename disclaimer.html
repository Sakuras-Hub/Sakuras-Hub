Privacy

The site has no ads, no analytics and no cookies. Your light or dark theme, favorites, recently visited sites (never adult ones) and a dismissed safety tip are saved in your own browser and nowhere else. The Recently visited list can be cleared with its Clear button.

Vercel hosts the site and may log standard request data, such as your IP address. A few features load data from other services, and those services see your IP address like any website you visit: GitHub (the Directory list and Script Hub data), Cloudflare (the Monitor), GuerrillaMail (the throwaway inbox in the Suit) and weao.xyz (live executor status).

Report a link or ask for a removal

If you own a listed site and want it removed, open a ticket (not the flag button) and tell me how to confirm it is yours. Removal requests from verified owners are honoured.
